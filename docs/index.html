What the report can and cannot say

  • Candidates only: confirm with the table owner. No reads observed does not prove a table is unused; shares, infrequent jobs and retention duties may be outside the evidence.
  • Per-table read history needs Snowflake Enterprise Edition or higher; the Standard Edition query-text fallback has lower confidence and misses reads through views.
  • In our trial, a count-only query left no object-level read record, so a table people only count can look unread.
  • Names and coverage need checking: renamed tables, incorrect mappings or incomplete history can hide reads.
  • A logged event in the same connection and month as a row increase does not prove its cause.
  • Prices are your own; estimates are not invoice guarantees, and individual and combined removal scenarios are alternatives, never additive.

Your exports stay with you

Do not email your exports.
